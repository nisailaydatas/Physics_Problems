Task 1: Displacement and Average Velocity

Problem: An object moves from A(2, 3) to B(5, 7) in 2 seconds.

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. Displacement ($\Delta r$): $\sqrt{(5-2)^2 + (7-3)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$ meters.

2. Average Velocity ($V_{avg}$): $\Delta r / \Delta t = 5m / 2s = 2.5$ m/s.

3. Unit Conversion: $2.5 \times 3.6 = 9$ km/h.
