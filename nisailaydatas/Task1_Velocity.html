Physics Basics L5 - Task 1

Problem Statement:
An object moves from point \( A(2, 3) \) to point \( B(5, 7) \) in \( 2 \) seconds. Calculate the displacement and the average velocity of the object.
1. Displacement (\(\Delta r\)):
Using the Pythagorean theorem:
$$\Delta r = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$ $$\Delta r = \sqrt{(5 - 2)^2 + (7 - 3)^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5 \text{ meters}$$
2. Average Velocity (\(V_{avg}\)):
$$V_{avg} = \frac{\Delta r}{\Delta t} = \frac{5 \text{ m}}{2 \text{ s}} = 2.5 \text{ m/s}$$
3. Unit Conversion:
To convert m/s to km/h, we multiply by 3.6:
$$2.5 \text{ m/s} \times 3.6 = 9 \text{ km/h}$$
