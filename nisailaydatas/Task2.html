Task 2: Constant Acceleration Dynamics

"An object starts from rest (\( v_0 = 0 \)) and accelerates at a rate of \( a = 2 \text{ m/s}^2 \) for \( t = 3 \text{ s} \). Calculate final velocity and displacement."

Mathematical Derivation

1. Final Velocity (\(v_f\)):

$$v_f = v_0 + (a \cdot t)$$ $$v_f = 0 + (2 \cdot 3) = \mathbf{6 \text{ m/s}}$$

2. Distance Traveled (\(s\)):

$$s = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2} a t^2$$ $$s = 0 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 3^2 = \mathbf{9 \text{ meters}}$$
