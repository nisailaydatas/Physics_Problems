Task 2: Motion with Constant Acceleration

Task: An object starts from rest (\(v_0 = 0\)) and accelerates at \(a = 2 \, \text{m/s}^2\) for \(t = 3 \, \text{s}\). Calculate \(v_f\) and \(d\).
Calculations:
1. Final Velocity: \(v_f = v_0 + a \cdot t = 0 + 2 \cdot 3 = \mathbf{6 \, \text{m/s}}\)
2. Distance: \(d = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2} a t^2 = 0 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 3^2 = \mathbf{9 \, \text{meters}}\)
