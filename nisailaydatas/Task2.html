Physics Basics L5 - Task 2

Problem Statement:
An object starts from rest (\( v_0 = 0 \)) and accelerates at a constant rate of \( a = 2 \text{ m/s}^2 \) for \( t = 3 \text{ s} \). Find the final velocity and the total distance traveled.
1. Final Velocity (\(v_f\)):
Using the kinematic equation:
$$v_f = v_0 + a \cdot t$$ $$v_f = 0 + (2 \text{ m/s}^2 \cdot 3 \text{ s}) = 6 \text{ m/s}$$
2. Distance Traveled (\(d\)):
Using the displacement formula:
$$d = v_0 \cdot t + \frac{1}{2} a \cdot t^2$$ $$d = 0 + \frac{1}{2} (2) \cdot (3)^2 = 9 \text{ meters}$$
